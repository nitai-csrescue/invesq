Updated feedback after reviewing the latest branding changes:

The new INVESQ logo and favicon direction are much stronger and feel substantially more institutional and product-oriented. The analytical “Q” concept is working well and communicates operational intelligence clearly.

A few refinement recommendations:

1. Increase favicon prominence in the navbar
   The favicon/icon itself is strong, but it currently feels visually small relative to the rest of the header. Increase size slightly and tighten alignment with the INVESQ wordmark.

2. Reduce visual competition in the hero
   The logo is now sophisticated enough to act as a primary visual anchor. Avoid overly busy supporting graphics or excessive gradients around it.

3. Improve typography hierarchy
   The logo feels enterprise-grade now, but some of the surrounding site typography still feels startup-template oriented.
   Suggestions:

* slightly heavier headers
* more spacing between sections
* fewer all-caps labels
* tighter max-width on paragraph text

4. Tighten hero copy further
   The design now feels more institutional than the current messaging.
   Suggested refinement:

Headline:
“Operational Due Diligence for Modern Investment Firms”

Subheadline:
“INVESQ helps PE and VC firms uncover hidden customer journey, adoption, and operational risks before capital is committed.”

5. Add more “financial systems intelligence” feel
   The logo implies analytical rigor and operational visibility. The rest of the site should reinforce that visually:

* cleaner charts
* structured cards
* dashboard-like confidence
* less generic AI imagery
* more operational workflow visuals

6. Improve the workflow visualization
   The current workflow/process section still feels slightly linear and generic.
   Would recommend:

* cleaner node-based architecture
* enterprise systems icons
* CRM / support / BI / communications inputs
* executive risk output cards

7. Make the product feel more proprietary
   The branding is now strong enough that the site should imply:
   “This is a category-defining diligence product.”

Ways to reinforce this:

* proprietary methodology language
* “Operational Intelligence Engine”
* “Customer Journey Reconstruction”
* “System-Level Validation”
* “Operational Risk Scoring”

8. Add one concrete sample insight
   Right now the site is still somewhat conceptual.
   Add a mock insight example such as:
   “Detected: 38% support escalation increase among top ARR accounts despite reported NRR growth.”

This immediately makes the product feel real.

9. Reduce consulting-firm tone
   The site should feel more like:
   Bloomberg + Palantir + PE operating diligence

Less like:
traditional advisory services or RevOps consulting.

10. Mobile responsiveness
    The new favicon/logo system will likely look excellent on mobile if:

* logo scales slightly larger
* hero padding reduced
* CTA stays above fold
* workflow stacks vertically cleanly
