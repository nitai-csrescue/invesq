Build a simple clickable web app demo called “CS Rescue for CEATI” that showcases how CEATI could view membership intelligence in a clean, executive-friendly operating workspace.

Goal:
Show a theoretical but realistic way CEATI could use CS Rescue to turn HubSpot, Mixpanel, Webex, Teams, Research Portal, and Event Platform data into simple weekly actions.

Style:
Modern, clean, minimal. More like Linear, Attio, Notion, or Vanta — not a traditional BI dashboard. Avoid cluttered charts. Use cards, clear hierarchy, soft borders, concise copy, and an executive command-center feel.

App Structure:
Create 5 main tabs/pages:

1. Overview
2. Risk
3. Growth
4. Account Brief
5. Actions

Global Header:
Title: CS Rescue · CEATI Operating Review
Subtitle: Weekly membership intelligence powered by BackEngine + AI reasoning
Date: Week of June 8, 2026
Badge: Illustrative demo data

Page 1 — Overview:
Show four main metric cards:
- Member Health Score: 72/100, +3 vs last week
- Value Realization: 68%, +2 pts MoM
- Expansion Opportunities: 23, 8 high confidence
- Utilities Needing Attention: 11, 3 critical

Below the cards, show an AI Executive Summary:
“Portfolio health improved this week, but risk is concentrated in 11 utilities. Cascade Power and Meridian Grid show sponsor-change plus declining research engagement, while Northshore Hydro is the strongest cross-program expansion opportunity.”

Add a small “Data Sources” row:
HubSpot, Mixpanel, Webex, Teams, Research Portal, Event Platform.

Page 2 — Risk:
Show a prioritized list of at-risk utilities.

Each risk card should include:
- Utility name
- Health score
- Trend
- Primary risk signals
- Source evidence
- Recommended next action

Use these sample utilities:
1. Cascade Power Authority — Health 41, down 14
Signals: sponsor changed, event attendance down 58%, portal logins down 71%, program participation 2 → 1
Action: Schedule executive re-engagement this week

2. Meridian Grid Services — Health 48, down 9
Signals: research portal use down 62%, no touchpoints in 30 days, active in only 2 of 5 enrolled programs
Action: Send value recap and book QBR

3. Lakeshore Energy — Health 52, down 6
Signals: no QBR in 9 months, skipped Generation Summit, no new report access in 60 days
Action: Trigger renewal check-in

Make every insight traceable with labels like HubSpot, Webex, Research Portal, Event Platform.

Page 3 — Growth:
Show top expansion opportunities in a clean table/card layout.

Columns:
- Utility
- Recommended Program
- Readiness Score
- Why Now
- Suggested Action

Use:
- Northshore Hydro → Asset Management, 92, attended 6 of 7 T&D events and downloaded asset lifecycle benchmark twice, Action: introduce Asset Management program
- Summit Energy Co-op → Cyber Security, 88, OT/IT initiative signals and QBR upcoming, Action: include Cyber Security in QBR
- Cascade Power Authority → Power Quality, 84, reliability questions in portal, Action: send Power Quality case study
- Pinecrest Utilities → Distribution Engineering, 81, peer cohort attending, Action: invite to next working group

Add a simple insight card:
“Utilities engaging across multiple CEATI programs are more likely to renew and expand. CS Rescue flags adjacent program interest before it becomes an explicit sales conversation.”

Interactivity:
- Tabs should navigate between pages.
- Risk cards should expand/collapse to show source evidence.
- Action cards should have status pills: Not Started, In Progress, Done.
- Account Brief should have a button labeled “Generate Briefing” that visually refreshes or highlights the briefing.
- Add a simple “Sync to HubSpot Task” button on action cards, but it does not need real integration.

Important Positioning:
This is not a replacement for HubSpot, Mixpanel, Webex, Teams, or existing workflows.
Show CS Rescue as a read-only intelligence layer powered by BackEngine integrations and AI reasoning.

Use only illustrative demo data. Make the UI polished enough to show in a CEATI follow-up call.
